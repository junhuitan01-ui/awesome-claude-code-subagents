# Deliverable specification

## Files to deliver
- `report.docx` — main written report
- `report.pdf` — PDF copy of the report

## Report requirements
- **Length:** 1,500–2,500 words
- **Required sections (in order):** Executive summary; What makes a major project strong; Additional ideas; How to choose and scope your additions; Risks and how to manage them; How this report was made (the debate result); Limitations; References
- **Must include:** a table of ideas with columns Idea | Why it helps | Effort (low / medium / high) | Sources; a short step-by-step method for prioritising ideas

## Style
- **Style guide:** `style-guide.md`
- **Citation style:** numbered [1] with reference list
- **Branding:** none
