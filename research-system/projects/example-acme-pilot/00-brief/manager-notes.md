# Pilot issues log (FICTIONAL TEST DATA)

Kept by the operations manager, January–June 2026.

- **January:** Two-week adjustment period. Production planning moved to a Monday–Thursday schedule. Small dip in output while shifts were re-planned.
- **February:** Meetings cut from 30 to 15 minutes by default; weekly all-hands moved to a written update.
- **March:** No major issues.
- **April:** Customer service raised concerns: Friday cover is one person on a rota and phone queue times rose. No extra hire was made.
- **May:** Best month for output and on-time delivery.
- **June:** Spike in customer complaints (18). Most related to slow Friday responses during a product recall notice that went out on a Thursday. Operations manager judged this mostly a one-off but linked to thin Friday cover.
- **Overall:** Production and warehouse teams adapted well. Customer service is the weak point; recommend fixing Friday cover before any permanent change.
