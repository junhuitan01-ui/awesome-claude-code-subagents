# Staff wellbeing survey summary (FICTIONAL TEST DATA)

Acme Widgets Ltd, prepared by HR, 5 July 2026.

Survey run twice: December 2025 (before pilot, 41 of 50 staff responded, 82%) and June 2026 (end of pilot, 37 of 51 staff responded, 73%). Same questions both times; answers on a 1–5 scale (5 = best). Anonymous, so individual changes cannot be tracked.

| Measure | Dec 2025 average | Jun 2026 average |
|---|---|---|
| Overall job satisfaction | 3.2 | 3.9 |
| Feeling of burnout (5 = no burnout) | 2.7 | 3.6 |
| Work-life balance | 2.9 | 4.1 |
| Workload is manageable | 3.1 | 3.0 |
| Would recommend Acme as an employer | 3.4 | 4.0 |

By team (overall job satisfaction, June 2026): Production 4.1 (n=19), Warehouse 4.0 (n=10), Customer service 3.1 (n=8).

Free-text comments: the most common theme (14 comments) was "more time with family / for rest". The second most common (6 comments, 5 from customer service) was that Friday cover was stressful and customers waited longer for replies.
