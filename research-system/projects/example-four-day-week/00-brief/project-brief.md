# Project brief

## Project name
example-four-day-week

## Purpose
Help a small company's leadership team decide whether to run a six-month four-day work week pilot.

## Audience
Company directors with no HR research background. They want a clear, balanced summary and a recommendation.

## Research questions
1. What did the major four-day week trials (for example the UK 2022 pilot and similar trials elsewhere) find about productivity, revenue and staff wellbeing?
2. What problems or limitations did those trials report, including how reliable their methods were?
3. What practical conditions seemed to make a trial succeed or fail?

## Scope
- **In scope:** Published results of organised four-day week trials since 2019.
- **Out of scope:** Compressed hours (40 hours in 4 days); legal advice.
- **Time period / geography:** 2019–present; any country.

## Sources
- **Preferred or required sources:** Official trial reports, academic analyses, reputable news coverage of those reports.
- **Sources to avoid:** Vendor marketing, opinion blogs.
- **Files provided:** none

## Success criteria
- [ ] Answers all three research questions with cited evidence
- [ ] States the limitations of the evidence (e.g. self-selected participants)
- [ ] Gives a clear recommendation with conditions
- [ ] Every number has a citation

## Constraints
- **Deadline:** none
- **Length / effort limits:** short — this is a test run
- **Other:** none
